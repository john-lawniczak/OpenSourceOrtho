FDI numbering: 11–18 = upper right, 21–28 = upper left; 31–38 = lower left, 41–48 = lower right. The last digit counts positions from the midline. 18 is an upper right wisdom-tooth position, not a lower tooth. Numbers describe possible positions, not a confirmed inventory of this person’s teeth.
